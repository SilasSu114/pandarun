# Glossary

## 熊猫卫士

The player character: an older antivirus program that has been mistaken for malware by the modern system.

## 逆转协议

The working title of the game.

## 隔离区

The quarantine space where Panda Guardian is trapped after being flagged as malicious.

## 系统核心

The ultimate destination where Panda Guardian can verify its identity.

## 场景旋转

The core 90-degree clockwise rotation that changes the visible space and the direction in which movable objects fall.

## 重力变换

The gameplay consequence of scene rotation: movable objects are affected by the new gravity direction.

## 通关条件物

The level-specific object that must be obtained before the exit becomes usable.

## 出口

The end-of-level point that only accepts the player after the condition object is obtained.

## 机关

An interactive level object that can change access or puzzle state.

## 可推动物体

A movable puzzle object that can be pushed by the player and is affected by gravity.

## 扫描光线

The Level 2 hazard that sweeps through the space and must be avoided.

## Demo 彩蛋

The preserved tutorial demo, kept inside the formal project as a hidden reward.

## Y2K 复古电脑系统

The retro computer aesthetic used across the game's presentation.
